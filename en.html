Mission

From source to decision, one community

Fabric brings together roles that used to work apart. The club brings together the people who do that work.

Club Fabric et IA Québec brings together professionals and organizations using Microsoft Power BI, Microsoft Fabric and AI agents applied to data.

Members help each other through demos, project debriefs and hands-on practices covering ingestion, transformation, modelling, visualization, governance and intelligent querying of data.

The club carries on from the Montreal Modern Excel and Power BI community and broadens its scope.

AI, an official focus of the club

Agents are now part of everyday data work

Power BI and Fabric already include AI. The club also covers how agents are designed and governed, how to use them responsibly, and how they change the way we work.

  • Agents that query Power BI semantic models
  • Copilot in Power BI and Microsoft Fabric
  • Copilot Studio and integration with Microsoft 365 or Teams
  • Coding agents for SQL, DAX, Power Query, Python and Fabric pipelines
  • Preparing and governing the data that agents use
  • Security, permissions, cost, answer quality and human accountability
  • Real-world results on productivity gains and observed limits

Principles

How the club works

  • Real projects and demos come before theory.
  • Meetups stay beginner-friendly; advanced sessions are clearly flagged.
  • Members are invited to present their projects and suggest topics.
  • Programming is shared among several hosts and speakers.
  • A code of conduct applies to every activity.
  • Rules for sponsorship and commercial content are public and transparent.
